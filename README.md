Yardline — a 5v5 round-based tactical FPS in a sun-baked container yard. Plant the charge at Crane Bay or Stack Yard, buy your loadout each freeze phase, and outplay five bots who follow team plans, hold sites, call out contacts on the radio, and rotate on the plant. First to 7 rounds takes the match.

Three lanes (Long Haul, Mid Yard, Tunnel), two bombsites, a buy economy, and a rifle, SMG, DMR, shotgun, pistol and knife — all modelled from real silhouettes, all textures painted procedurally, all sounds synthesized in WebAudio. No external assets, no network requests.

## Play

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome.

## Controls

- **W A S D** — move · **Mouse** — look & fire · **R** — reload
- **1 / 2 / 3** — rifle / sidearm / knife · **4 / 5 / 6** — frag / smoke / flash
- **E** — plant / defuse (hold) · **Ctrl** — crouch · **Space** — jump
